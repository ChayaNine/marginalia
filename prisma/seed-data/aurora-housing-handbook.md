# Aurora Campus Housing — Resident Handbook

Welcome to Aurora Campus Housing. This handbook explains how the buildings run, what we expect from residents, and what residents can expect from us. It applies to all four residence halls: Birch House, Cedar House, Larch House and Rowan House.

## 1. Moving in

Move-in weekend is the Saturday and Sunday before the autumn term begins. Each resident receives a move-in slot by email two weeks in advance; slots are two hours long and are assigned by floor so that lifts are not overloaded. Trolleys can be borrowed from reception against a photo ID.

On arrival, residents collect a key card and a room inventory form. The inventory form must be checked, signed and returned to reception within 48 hours of moving in. Any damage not recorded on the form at that point will be treated as caused by the resident.

Rooms are provided with a bed, mattress, desk, chair, wardrobe, bookshelf and curtains. Residents bring their own bedding, towels, crockery and cutlery. Kettles and toasters are permitted only in the shared kitchens, never in bedrooms.

## 2. Rent and deposit

Rent is charged termly and is due on the first day of each term. Payment can be made by bank transfer or through the resident portal. A late payment fee of £25 applies to any payment received more than 14 days after the due date.

A deposit equal to four weeks' rent is paid before arrival. The deposit is held with a government-approved tenancy deposit scheme and is refunded within 30 days of move-out, less any agreed deductions for damage or cleaning beyond normal wear and tear. Residents receive an itemised statement of any deductions.

## 3. Quiet hours and noise

Quiet hours run from 11 pm to 7 am on weekdays and from midnight to 8 am on Fridays and Saturdays. During quiet hours, noise must not be audible outside a resident's own room. Outside quiet hours, residents are still expected to keep noise to a level that does not disturb neighbours.

During the examination period, which is announced by email each term, quiet hours are extended to run from 9 pm to 8 am every day. Musical instruments may be played only in the practice room on the ground floor of Cedar House, which can be booked through the portal in one-hour blocks.

## 4. Guests

Residents may host guests between 8 am and midnight without prior arrangement. A guest may stay overnight for up to three consecutive nights, and no more than ten nights in any calendar month, provided the resident registers the guest at reception before 10 pm on the first night. Residents are responsible for their guests' behaviour at all times.

Guests are not permitted during the examination period except with written permission from the Residence Manager.

## 5. Pets and animals

No pets are permitted in the residence halls, with two exceptions. First, residents may keep a single small aquarium of up to 20 litres in their own room. Second, assistance animals are welcome; residents should notify the accommodation office before arrival so that suitable arrangements can be made.

## 6. Kitchens, cleaning and waste

Each floor has a shared kitchen with a fridge-freezer, oven, hob, microwave and dishwasher. Residents are responsible for cleaning up after themselves immediately after use. Contractors deep-clean each shared kitchen every Tuesday morning between 9 am and 11 am; anything left on worktops at that time will be removed.

Bedrooms are cleaned by residents. Vacuum cleaners are available from the cleaning cupboard on each floor. General waste, recycling and food waste must be separated using the labelled bins in the kitchen. Bin stores are located at the rear of each hall.

## 7. Maintenance and repairs

Maintenance requests are submitted through the resident portal under "Report a problem". Requests are acknowledged within one business day, and routine repairs are completed within five business days. Emergency issues — a leak, a power failure, a broken external door, or anything that makes a room unsafe — should be reported by phone to the duty team on 0300 555 0142, which is staffed 24 hours a day.

Maintenance staff will always knock and announce themselves before entering a room. Except in an emergency, residents receive at least 24 hours' notice of any planned entry.

## 8. Safety and security

Key cards must not be lent to anyone. A lost card must be reported to reception immediately; a replacement costs £15. External doors must never be propped open.

Fire alarms are tested at 10 am every Wednesday; there is no need to evacuate during the test. At any other time, residents must leave the building by the nearest exit when the alarm sounds and assemble at the marked assembly point in the car park. Candles, incense and any device with an open flame are prohibited in all halls. Smoking and vaping are not permitted anywhere inside the buildings or within five metres of any entrance.

## 9. Internet and laundry

Wired and wireless internet is included in the rent. Each room has an Ethernet socket; the wireless network is named AuroraResidents and the password is printed on the back of the key card holder. Personal routers and range extenders are not permitted because they interfere with the campus network.

Laundry rooms are on the ground floor of each hall and are open from 7 am to 11 pm. Machines are operated through the laundry app; a wash costs £3.20 and a dry costs £1.60. Clothes left in a machine for more than 30 minutes after a cycle ends may be moved to the shelves by other residents.

## 10. Post and parcels

Letters are delivered to the pigeonholes at reception. Parcels are signed for by reception and residents are notified by email; parcels must be collected within seven days, after which they are returned to the sender. Reception is open from 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and from 10 am to 4 pm at weekends.

## 11. Moving out

Residents must vacate their rooms by 10 am on the last day of the contract. Rooms must be left clean and empty, with all furniture returned to its original position. A member of staff inspects each room against the signed inventory form within three days of departure. Key cards are returned to the drop box at reception; an unreturned card is charged at the replacement rate of £15.

Residents who wish to stay over the summer can apply for a summer contract through the portal; applications open in April and are allocated in order of receipt.

## 12. Complaints and contacts

Concerns about another resident should be raised first with the floor's Resident Assistant. Formal complaints are made in writing to the Residence Manager, who responds within ten business days. The accommodation office can be reached at housing@aurora-campus.example or on 0300 555 0100 during office hours.
